01 · What changed

New org charts are being drawn.
With more kinds of co-workers in the boxes.

At Frontier Firms, processes get drawn around the work that actually happens. An agent can own a step where three humans used to share it. Or the other way around. Titles follow later.

Section two
02

What sets them apart.

Two things. And they contradict each other.

02 · What sets them apart
01

They think in flows first.
Titles come later.

An agent doesn't care whose title a piece of work used to belong to. It does work. And Frontier Firms draw the process around that. People get to do what they're best at, rather than what their title prescribes.

02 · What sets them apart
02

They give the agent a manager.
And a measurable outcome.

At Frontier Firms, the agent gets onboarding like any new hire. It gets a role and an owner. Plus a clear outcome the owner reviews every week. Banal, sure. And the biggest difference between companies that move and companies that just experiment.
